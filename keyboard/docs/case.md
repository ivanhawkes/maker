# Case Design: Keyboard 1

Status: **v2 generated 2026-11-23 for the v4 boards, digital checks only.** Nothing is printed, no part has been
measured, and no fit is proven. Every dimension comes from a datasheet or a product page, with
slack added, because the parts are not here yet (owner, 2026-11-23). This is the modern black
case of [decisions.md](decisions.md), with its [tenting wedges](#tenting-wedges-2026-11-23); the retro case is not started.

The case is generated by `tools/case_generate.py` from `layout/layout.yaml` and the two board
files, and checked against both boards on every run. The files are in `cad/case/`.

## What It Is

Three printed parts a half, and no fasteners on show:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tray** | Floor, walls, 26 posts under the board and a wider one under each of its four mounting holes, the battery bay. The board drops in from above |
| **Plate** | 1.6 mm switch plate, inlaid flush with the tray's rim. Two M3 × 8 screws come up through the board's holes H1 and H2 into bosses under it, and its outer edge hooks under a dovetail in the rim |
| **Lid** | Raised cover over the controller, battery connector and battery. Four M3 × 12 screws from below hold it: two in the battery lobe, and two through the board's holes H3 and H4 into bosses that stand on the board. Its skirt clamps the plate's inner edge |

In plan the case is the board's outline plus 4 mm, with a lobe on the inner edge that holds the
battery beside the board. It is 203.7 × 124.8 mm. The key deck is flat and the keys stand
proud of it; the lid is a step 3.6 mm above the deck along the inner edge.

## Decisions And Reasons

- **Battery: Core Electronics CE04375**, 400 mAh, 38 × 25 × 6 mm, 11 g, JST-PH, with a
  protection board (product page read 2026-11-23). The owner asked for a cell from Core
  Electronics; its two-pin range is 120 mAh (28 × 13 × 5), this one, and 1100 mAh and up
  (51 × 34 × 6 and larger). The 400 mAh cell is the largest that fits without a much bigger
  case. Listed with a 30 to 40 day lead time, road freight only. To be revisited when a cell is
  in hand. Runtime is not estimated here: [architecture.md](architecture.md) asks for measured
  current first.
- **The cell sits beside the board, not under or over it.** Under the board it would have to
  go below the sockets, making the whole case about 7 mm taller, or under the controller, where
  the antenna is. Over the board there is no free area 38 × 25 mm clear of the keycaps and the
  antenna. Beside it, the case stays 11.6 mm tall and grows about 30 mm wider on the inner side,
  where nothing is in the way of a hand.
- **The bay is 42.5 × 28.5 mm and 7.0 mm high under the lid's two ribs**: 4.5, 3.5 and 1.0 mm
  more than the cell, for the protection board, the leads, differences between the product
  page and the real cell, and swelling. Pad a loose cell with foam tape. It is 17.9 mm from the
  antenna end of the controller.
- **The plate and lid print top face down**, so the visible faces take the bed's texture.
  Nothing can then stand above a part's top face, which is why the raised cover is its own part.
- **Four screws go through the board** (its mounting holes, added in board v4 for this case).
  Each clamps the board between a post in the tray and a boss above: under the plate at H1 (top
  left) and H2 (bottom middle), under the lid at H3 (beside the USB notch) and H4 (above the
  outer thumb key). The dovetail on the plate's outer edge stays, because no hole fits at the
  board's bottom left corner, where the keys reach the edge.
- **Screws cut their own thread** in 2.6 mm holes: 4.2 mm of engagement under the plate, 7.8 mm
  in the lid's bosses on the board, 2.8 mm in its two lobe bosses. Every tip stops 0.8 mm under
  the visible face. For heat-set inserts, change `PILOT_R` and the bosses.
- **Slicer profile as the design constraint** (owner, 2026-11-23): the one found in the
  prototype file, Bambu X2D, 0.4 mm nozzle, PETG Basic, 0.2 mm layers, 256 mm bed.

## Printing

Files in `cad/case/print/` are meshes already turned as they print: the tray floor down, the
plate and lid top face down. No supports are intended: the dovetail overhangs by 32° from
vertical, and the rest is vertical walls and open-topped slots.

- The screw-head recesses in the tray's underside are closed by a 0.2 mm skin, so that they
  bridge. Push a screw or a 3.5 mm drill through each.
- The outline's round corners are 0.005 mm polylines and the chamfers (tray bottom, lid top)
  are cut in 0.2 mm steps, one a layer. Both are workarounds for FreeCAD faults, noted in the
  generator. Printed at 0.2 mm they are the same as arcs and 45° chamfers.
- A plain mesh has no print settings. Open it in Bambu Studio and choose the profile there.

Hardware a half: four M3 × 12 and two M3 × 8 screws, with heads up to 6 mm across and 2.4 mm
high (button or pan heads; a socket cap head stands proud), and five stick-on feet up to 10 mm
across.

## Assembly (Proposed, Untested)

1. Clip the switches into the plate and press the soldered board onto them.
2. Put the cell in the bay and lead its wire through the slot.
3. Lower the plate and board into the tray outer edge first, at a tilt, so the plate's bevelled
   edge goes under the dovetail; then lay it flat. The power switch's slider drops into its slot.
4. Plug in the battery, after checking its polarity with a meter ([pcb.md](pcb.md)).
5. Fit the lid, then the six screws from below: the two short ones at H1 and H2, under the
   plate's top left and bottom middle.

## Generating And Checking

```sh
"$PY" tools/case_board.py           # KiCad's Python (see pcb.md, working notes): board data
FreeCADCmd tools/case_generate.py   # about 3 minutes; exit 1 if a check fails
```

`tools/case_board.py` writes `cad/case/board-left.json` and `board-right.json`: the outline,
and each footprint's position and courtyard. The generator refuses them if the board file has
changed since. It builds the left half, mirrors it, and writes:

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `<side>-tray.step`, `-plate.step`, `-lid.step` | The parts, in the layout's coordinates, z = 0 at the board's top |
| `<side>-case.FCStd` | The three parts with the board, its parts and the keycaps as blocks, for viewing |
| `print/<side>-<part>.3mf` | Meshes for printing |
| `report.txt` | The results of the last run; removed at the start of a run, so a run that stops early leaves none |

**Results, 2026-11-23** (FreeCAD 1.1.4, KiCad 10.0.8; `cad/case/report.txt`). All pass, on
both halves:

- Each part is one valid solid and fits the bed. No two parts overlap.
- No part touches the board or anything on it. The board's parts are modelled as blocks: each
  footprint's courtyard, 2.0 mm deep under the board; the controller 18.2 × 35 × 6.5 mm; the
  connector 5.2 mm, reset 2.2 mm and power switch 1.8 mm high with its slider; 28 switches;
  the cell. The right half is checked against the right board, whose underside is not a mirror
  of the left's.
- The right board's outline and the positions of its controller, connector and switches are
  the left's mirrored, so one mirrored case serves.
- 28 switch cutouts. The lid is 1.30 mm from the nearest keycap (the inner column's; 1.7 mm
  from the long thumb caps, taken as 18 × 27 mm).
- Every post is at least 0.85 mm from any part's courtyard on either board.
- Each mesh is closed and has its solid's volume: tray 61.9, plate 13.4, lid 9.6 cm³.

## Limits And Open Items

Found while designing; none is tested.

- **Fits are guesses**: 0.2 mm around the plate, 0.8 mm around the board, the dovetail. Print
  one half and adjust before printing the other.
- **The plate's bottom left corner** is the furthest from a screw (about 64 mm from H2); only
  the dovetail holds that edge.
- **Thin sections at H3.** The lid has 1.3 mm of wall between the screw and its face towards
  the keys, and the board 0.9 mm between the hole and the USB notch. To leave that wall the
  lid stands 1.3 mm from the inner column's keycaps, not 1.7.
- **USB plug.** The tunnel is 13 mm wide and the notch gives the plug 2 mm below the board's
  top. A plug wider than 13 mm does not fit.
- **Power switch.** The slider's tip is level with the floor of a 16 mm recess: a fingertip
  should move it, but that is untested.
- **Battery connector.** About 6.5 mm between the header and the lid's skirt for the plug and
  the bend in the lead. Check with a real cell.
- **Battery safety is not reviewed.** No cell datasheet is published, only the protection
  board's (DW01+, 4.275 V and 2.5 V cut-offs). The lead's polarity, the cell's charge limit
  against the controller's 100 mA, the fit in the bay, pinching of the lead under the lid, and
  temperature while charging in a closed PETG bay are all to check before use.
- **Radio.** The cell is 17.9 mm from the controller's antenna end; the effect is unknown.
- **Elsewhere:** the retro case for keyboard 2 is in [case-retro.md](case-retro.md), built round this one's insides; the dongle's case is in [dongle-case.md](dongle-case.md).

## Tenting Wedges (2026-11-23)

Status: **generated, digital checks only.** Nothing is printed; comfort, grip and stability are
untested. The design was proposed by Claude and accepted by the owner before it was built.

A wedge is a cradle. The assembled case, feet and all, drops into a 3 mm rim that follows its
outline, and lifts out again: nothing is screwed and the tray is unchanged. The wedge raises
the inner edge only. There is one for each half at 5°, 10° and 15°, the trial angles of
[decisions.md](decisions.md).

| Angle | Case's outer edge above the desk | Inner edge | Size as printed | Solid volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5° | 5.2 mm | 23.0 mm | 210.5 × 130.2 × 26.2 mm | 82.9 cm³ |
| 10° | 5.5 mm | 40.8 mm | 213.7 × 130.2 × 44.3 mm | 132.6 cm³ |
| 15° | 5.7 mm | 58.4 mm | 218.5 × 130.2 × 62.0 mm | 182.5 cm³ |

- **Rim:** 2.4 mm thick, 3 mm high, 0.3 mm from the case all round. Its top is 1.6 mm below the
  tray's lowest opening (the USB tunnel), so it needs no cut-outs for the plug, the power
  switch or the reset hole.
- **Seat:** the case's floor rests on a 3 mm ledge inside the rim and on 2.4 mm ribs, about
  3400 mm². Between them the wedge is open from top to bottom, so it prints without supports.
- **Pockets for the case's feet:** 13 mm across and 3.5 mm deep, at the tray's five foot
  recesses, so feet up to 12 mm across and 3 mm proud can stay on.
- **Its own feet:** six recesses for 10 mm stick-on feet, as on the tray, at the edge of the
  base: under the case's five, and one more under the high far corner.
- **Walls** are square to the case, so they lean with it, and the base reaches past the case's
  inner edge by the lean (about 16 mm at 15°).
- **Printing:** base down, as exported. The lean is at most 15° and needs no support. The seat
  is a shallow slope and will show layer steps, under the case.

```sh
FreeCADCmd tools/wedge_generate.py   # about 1 minute; exit 1 if a check fails
```

It reads `cad/case/left-tray.step`, so run it after `tools/case_generate.py`. It builds the left
wedge and mirrors it, and writes `<side>-wedge-<angle>.step`, `print/<side>-wedge-<angle>.3mf`
and `wedge-report.txt` in `cad/case/`. In the STEP files the desk is z = 0.

**Results, 2026-11-23** (FreeCAD 1.1.4; `cad/case/wedge-report.txt`). All pass, at each angle:

- One valid solid that fits the bed; each mesh is closed and has its solid's volume.
- The tray sits in it without overlap, and the rim stops it moving 1 mm in any of four
  directions.
- A press on any key, straight down or along the key's axis, lands inside the wedge's feet.
- The centre of mass of tray and wedge is over the feet.

Not checked, and to find out by printing (the 10° wedge first):

- Whether a 3 mm rim, of which 2 mm bears on the tray's wall above its chamfer, holds the case
  when it is knocked. The case is only held by its weight.
- Grip on the desk, flex, and whether the 0.3 mm fit is right.
- The lid, plate, keycaps and battery are not in the centre-of-mass check, only the tray.
- A plug in the USB port, and the cable's pull on a tilted half.

## Board Changes Made For The Case (2026-11-23)

The owner asked for all three changes that v1 of this case suggested. They are in board v4
([pcb.md](pcb.md#v4-notches-and-mounting-holes-2026-11-23)), and this case is built on them:

1. **A notch under the USB plug**, 13 mm wide, back to 16.0 mm from the controller's centre.
2. **Four mounting holes**, M3, not plated. The round shoulder that v1 had at the far inner
   corner is gone. The dovetail stays (above).
3. **More overhang for the power switch's slider: 1.85 mm, not the 2.5 mm proposed.** The
   switch's two locating pegs sit in holes that reach 0.78 mm from its centre, and the notch
   that gives the overhang must leave board beside them.
