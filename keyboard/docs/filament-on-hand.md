## What It Means For The Cases

- **The PETG on hand is PETG Basic**, the line the prototype was printed in and the cases'
  print profile is taken from ([decisions.md](decisions.md)). The colour candidates in
  [case-retro.md](case-retro.md) are from PETG HF, a different line with different colours:
  there is no Cream in this list, and PETG Basic's Orange (#FF671F) is not PETG HF's
  (#F75403).
- **Keyboard 1** (black): PETG Basic Black is on hand.
- **The retro case: decided (owner, 2026-11-24), Dark Beige for the body and Orange for the
  plate and stripe**, both from this list, with nothing bought. Dark Beige is greyer and less yellow
  than PETG HF Cream (#F9DFB9), nearer the off-white of the first renders (#E7E2D1).
- **PETG-CF Brick Red** is the nearest thing on hand to a burnt orange or rust, and prints
  matte. It is filled with carbon fibre, which wears a plain brass nozzle: the owner has a
  hardened steel one (2026-11-24). Still to check before it goes beside the board: it may not
  be a good insulator, and it can affect the radio if it is near the antenna. Neither is
  verified here.
- **Owner's preference (2026-11-24):** use the colours on hand if possible, and start with
  only those.
- The only PLA is Jade White; there is no PLA Matte, so a Terracotta accent would be bought.
- ABS is not what the cases are designed or profiled for.
