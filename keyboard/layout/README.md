# Layout Source

[`layout.yaml`](layout.yaml) is the editable source of truth for switch positions. It
defines the left half parametrically, and the right half is generated by mirroring.
Everything downstream (plate, case, PCB footprint placement, ZMK physical layout) should
be generated from it or checked against it, never edited by hand to diverge.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `layout.yaml` | Hand-edited source: columns (top key, angle, 19.05 mm pitch), and the thumb arc. One cutout size for every key |
| `generated/left-hand.csv`, `generated/right-hand.csv` | Per-key centre, rotation and cutout size. Generated; do not edit |
| `generated/layout.svg` | Plan view of both halves with cutouts and assumed 18 mm keycaps. Generated |
| [`../tools/layout.py`](../tools/layout.py) | Builds and checks the above |
| [`../tools/freecad_extract.py`](../tools/freecad_extract.py) | Read-only cutout extraction from a FreeCAD sketch (run by `check-cad`) |
| [`../cad/left-hand/plate.fcstd`](../cad/left-hand/plate.fcstd) | Corrected working CAD for the left plate (see below) |
| [`../tools/freecad_export.py`](../tools/freecad_export.py) | Exports the corrected CAD to `plate.step`, `plate-baseplate.dxf` and `plate.3mf`, and reads each back |

## Commands

Run these inside the devenv shell (it provides PyYAML and FreeCAD), from `keyboard/`:

```sh
python tools/layout.py build       # regenerate layout/generated/*
python tools/layout.py check       # 28 keys per half, no overlaps, exact mirror, outputs fresh
python tools/layout.py check-cad   # cad/left-hand/plate.fcstd must match the layout exactly
```

`check-cad` opens the file read-only and matches every cutout to a layout key within
0.001 mm, 0.001° and 0.001 mm cutout size. The prototype is not checked: it is kept as the
printed original only.

## Changing The Layout

The values in `layout.yaml` are pinned as the reference (owner, 2026-11-23). They are the
intended layout, not a copy of the printed prototype:

- Every switch cutout is the same size (`defaults.cutout`). There are no per-key sizes.
- Every column is four keys at one pitch along its own axis. The pinky columns C0 and C1 are
  turned 6° and are otherwise like the others: together they form a 19.05 mm grid turned 6°.
- The thumb keys are evenly spaced, 14° apart, on one arc.

To move something, edit a column's `top` or the thumb arc in `layout.yaml`, then run `build`
and `check`. Move C0 and C1 together to keep the pinky grid. `check-cad` fails for the moved
keys until the CAD is updated to match.

## Conventions

- Only the switch positions are the accepted layout. The prototype's plate outline, its
  1.6 mm thickness and the test rim were a rough template and may change in the case design
  (owner, 2026-11-22).
- Millimetres. Origin at the centre of the 170 × 122 mm prototype plate outline. The origin is
  kept as a fixed reference even if the plate outline changes.
- Plan view looking down on the keycaps: +x towards the user's right, +y away from the user.
- Rotation in degrees, counter-clockwise positive, about the key centre.
- Key ids: `C0`–`C5` are the columns from the outer pinky inwards, `R1` (number row) to
  `R4` (bottom alpha row); `T0`–`T3` are the thumb keys from the innermost outwards. Ids keep
  their meaning on the right half; full names are prefixed `L_` or `R_`.
- The right half is the mirror about the y axis: x → −x, rotation → −rotation. This is
  geometric only; PCB footprints, connectors and routing are not mirrored by it.

### Prototype CAD

`prototype/left-hand/test-pad.fcstd`, sha256
`112a3d754195efd7cbbab1613d77de8886c2ad3e6694e86f8da4b11793622767`, unchanged.

- **Contents:** only the 28 switch cutouts in the `Baseplate` sketch are intended (owner,
  2026-11-22). The `Rim` sketch and `RimPad` form a test box (4 mm wall, 6 mm deep) around
  the 170 × 122 mm plate and are not part of the layout. `BaseplatePad` makes a 1.6 mm plate.
- **Parameters:** the `VarSet` holds `gridSpacing` 19.05 mm, `keyholeSize` 14 mm,
  `pinkyAngle` 6°, `thumbRadius` 83 mm and `thumbAngle` 42° (the +14° to −28° sweep). Every
  column is 4 keys on a 19.05 mm pitch along its own axis. The thumb keys lie on an 83 mm arc
  centred 83 mm below T1, at +14°, 0°, −14° and −28°; these signs are confirmed from the cutout
  edges.
- **Defect:** C1R3 and C1R4 are sized with `DistanceX`/`DistanceY` (projected) constraints
  instead of the edge-length constraints the other 26 cutouts use. On the 6° column that
  makes their edges 14 / cos 6° = 14.07712 mm.
- **Knock-on shift:** the pinky cluster is anchored to C2R4 through a corner of C1R3
  (`DistanceX` 8.0, `DistanceY` 7.0), and C0 is tied to C1R4 (collinear edge plus
  `gridSpacing`). As a result the oversized squares also moved C0 by 0.109 mm and C1 by
  0.077 mm.

### Thumb Caps (2026-11-22)

- **Owner's physical test:** the printed prototype has 1u caps on T0 and T1 and 1.5u Thumb
  caps on **T2 and T3**, the two outermost thumb keys (the owner counts these as "T3 and T4").
  The 1.5u caps are fitted vertically, with the long side along the finger direction, and
  their stems are centred. A photo shows every cap seated and clear of its neighbours.
- **Calculation:** this used the true plan outlines of the published KLP Lamé STEP models
  (MX stem, MX size, commit `8e4b89b`), taken as the convex hull of each model's body. The
  1u Thumb cap is 17.99 × 17.99 mm and the 1.5u Thumb V cap is 17.98 × 26.99 mm, both with
  heavily rounded corners. Placed on the current layout, the minimum gaps are:

  | Pair | Gap |
  | --- | --- |
  | T2–T3 | 0.56 mm |
  | C5R4–T1 | 1.06 mm |
  | T1–T2 | 1.36 mm |
  | T0–T1 | 1.63 mm |

  Ordinary 1u neighbours clear by 1.06 mm. This agrees with the physical test.
- **Correction:** an earlier calculation modelled the caps as sharp-cornered rectangles. It
  wrongly predicted that every pair of 1.5u caps would collide, because all the predicted
  collisions were at corners. Do not use rectangles for thumb-cap clearance.
- **Owner's estimate:** the visible spacing between caps is closer to 2 mm (eyeballed, not
  measured). The figures above are at the cap base. The caps taper to about 15.75 mm at the
  top, so the gap you see from above is larger.
- **Deferred (owner, 2026-11-22):** add the real cap outlines and the 1.5u positions to
  `check`. The prototype proves the caps fit for now. Until this is done, `check` uses
  17.99 mm squares, which are conservative and report 0.18 mm on the thumb arc.
